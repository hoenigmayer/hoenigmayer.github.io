My Mission: Fostering excellent research and advancing Responsible Innovation

About

I help institutions navigate the ethical dimensions of science and technology. With a focus on responsible research and innovation (RRI), I design strategies that integrate ethical considerations into policy, ensuring that advancements in fields like AI, biotech, and sustainability are aligned with societal values and human rights.

My focus areas

  • Ethical Governance Creating frameworks for excellent research and innovation.
  • Science Policy Advising on policies that balance progress with ethical integrity.
  • Public Engagement Facilitating dialogue between scientists, policymakers, and the public to ensure societal value.
